# Huddable

Rearrange the vanilla HUD in game, no resource packs needed.

- Drag HUD elements to move them, with snapping to edges and other elements, chat included
- Scale elements from 25% to 400%, resize chat and bars by their edges, or hide them
- Place other mods' HUD elements too (anything added through Fabric API's HUD registry), and toasts, Fancy
  Notify's notifications included
- Place SkyCubed's and SkyOcean's overlays one by one (meowdding-lib overlays, can be turned off)
- Window, GUI Scale or Fixed sizing for the whole HUD
- Restyle health, hunger, armor and air as bars or numbers, in your own colours
- Put the scoreboard, chat, tab list and other HUD text in any font from your resource packs, per profile or
  per element, with a font quality setting
- Frame any element with your resource packs' tooltip, popup or button art, from the top pack or any enabled
  pack, per profile or per element
- Pick which resource pack's inventory, chest and other container art you see, for all screens or one by one
- Named layout profiles, shared as copy-paste codes
- Client-side, works on any server

Open the HUD Designer and General Settings from Mod Menu's Configure button. Requires Fabric API.
